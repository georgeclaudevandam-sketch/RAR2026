How accurate are the tracks?

Crew 204 (Team Oar Deal) was recorded three ways on race day. This page compares them second by second.

The three sources

YB tracker: the race's official satellite tracker, used for every crew on the main page.
Niobium: the crew's own log, one reading a second, with speed and stroke data.
Maptattoo: a phone GPS track, one point every 10 seconds.

Niobium is used as the reference throughout because it records most often. Where Niobium and Maptattoo, two independent devices, agree within a few metres, that is taken as the true track.

1. Do the clocks agree?

Each source was shifted in time to find the offset that best lines it up with Niobium, hour by hour. Zero means the clocks agree.

2. How far apart are the positions?

Distance from each source's fix to where Niobium put the boat at that same instant. "Along" is the error in the direction of travel (looks like a timing error); "across" is sideways error.

4. Gate and finish times

5. Leg distances

Distance rowed on each leg according to each source, using the same gate times.

6. Speed

Speed from positions compared with the Niobium speed reading, averaged over different windows. Bias is the average difference; 95% of differences fall within the range shown.
